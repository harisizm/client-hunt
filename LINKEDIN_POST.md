### Post Copy:

Our marketing and outreach team was spending 6 to 8 hours every single day manually grinding on Google Maps.

The routine was brutal:
- Search an area block by block
- Copy business name
- Copy phone number
- Copy address, rating, reviews, website
- Paste into a giant spreadsheet
- Realize half of them were duplicate contacts already called last week

A full day of exhausting manual copy-pasting yielded around 300 leads. It was slow, error-prone, and demoralizing for our sales reps.

So at Web 7 Labs, I decided to automate the entire pipeline from scratch.

Introducing **Client Hunt**: an autonomous, desktop-grade lead generation engine built to discover, scrape, verify, and export qualified business leads in minutes.

Here is what happened when we ran it:
What previously took our team a full 8-hour workday (300 leads) was collected in **just 10 minutes flat**.

Here is why this tool became a game-changer for our workflow:

1. **Autonomous Deep Discovery**
Instead of just scraping the top 20 surface results, Client Hunt runs smart coordinate expansion and multi-keyword wording passes around map coordinates. A search that manually yields 30 places produces 150+ to 200+ verified businesses automatically.

2. **Smart Deduplication with Transparent Reason Tags**
The nightmare of cold calling is calling the exact same business twice. Client Hunt tracks every known phone number and name in an embedded local SQLite engine. If a place already exists, it is marked with an explicit reason tag (e.g. "same phone as X in Y search"). You can choose whether to keep, highlight, or filter them out during export.

3. **Zero-Friction Setup (Double-Click & Run)**
No complicated cloud deployments, no Google Maps API keys with surprise monthly bills, and no complex coding environments for non-technical team members.
Setup is a one-time `.bat` file. To launch daily, the marketing team simply double-clicks `Start Client Hunt.bat` and the dashboard opens right up in their browser.

4. **Crash-Resilient & Local**
Every single record is saved live as it is discovered. If someone closes the laptop, pauses the search, or loses internet connection, hitting "Resume" picks up from the exact second it stopped.

5. **Instant 1-Click Excel & CSV Exports**
One sheet per neighborhood/area, pre-formatted bold filterable headers, frozen top rows, and direct Google Maps links ready for immediate CRM import or calling campaigns.
